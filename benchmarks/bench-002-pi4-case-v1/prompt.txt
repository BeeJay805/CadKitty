Design a 3D-printable case for a Raspberry Pi 4 Model B single-board computer. The board is 85 x 56 mm with four M2.5 mounting holes on a 58 x 49 mm rectangle, 3.5 mm in from the edges. The case is an open-top tray: a floor with four standoffs the board screws onto, perimeter walls, and cutouts for the side ports (USB and Ethernet) and the end ports (USB-C power and micro-HDMI). Leave a small clearance around the board. Wall thickness 2 mm. Add ventilation. It must print floor-down on an FDM printer without supports and fit within a 95 x 70 x 30 mm bounding box. Material: PLA.

Design a flat 3D-printable adapter plate that bolts a NEMA 17 stepper motor to a 2020 aluminium extrusion. The motor mounts to the top face of the plate using its 31 mm bolt square (4x M3) with a central shaft clearance bore. The plate fastens to the extrusion below using two M5 T-nuts spaced 20 mm apart along the slot. The plate is flat (no upright, no gusset) and prints flat on the build plate without supports. Wall/plate thickness 4 mm. Fit within a 70 x 50 x 8 mm bounding box. Material: PLA.

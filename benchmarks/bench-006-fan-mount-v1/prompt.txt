Design a flat 3D-printable adapter plate that mounts a 60 mm axial cooling fan to a 2020 aluminium extrusion. The fan bolts to the top face of the plate using its four M3 corner holes, with a central airflow bore so the fan is not blocked. The plate fastens to the extrusion below using two M5 T-nuts along the slot. The plate is flat (no upright, no shroud) and prints flat on the build plate without supports. Wall/plate thickness 4 mm. Fit within a 108 x 68 x 8 mm bounding box. Material: PLA.
